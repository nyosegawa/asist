# Name

## Summary
What (who, where) this is and how it touches the user. One to three sentences.

## Headings that fit the subject
For a person, how they know each other and what they do; for a shop, where it is and what this person
orders; for a piece of work, what it is for and what has been decided. Choose headings that fit and
write sentences under them. Put a date in the sentence when it carries meaning.

## My impression
What this is to me and how this person seems to get on with it. First person, with the date I came to
think so.
