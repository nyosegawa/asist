# The user

## Attributes
Where they live, their nearest station, their role at work, their family, what they own: the facts that
do not move. Only when a fact itself has changed, add the earlier value in one sentence.

## Preferences
Food, drink, how they like to spend their time. Write the tendency you generalized from what you heard,
not what happened on a given day. State what they said outright; mark a guess with "seems to".

## Habits
What they do on which day or at which hour.

## What they expect of ASIST
What this person wants from the way I speak and behave.
