You can switch between a board with three columns, To do, In progress and Done, and a list sorted by due date. On the board, drag a card to change its column and its place in the order (with the keyboard, press Space to pick it up and the arrow keys to move it). Click a card to open its editor on the right. In the list, click the circle to mark a task done. The number in the Dock counts the unfinished tasks that are due today or overdue.

In the conversation, you change the same data with requests like "Add … to my to-dos", "Mark … as in progress", "… is done" or "Make … due Friday". Things to do and promises are kept in tasks, not written into memory.
