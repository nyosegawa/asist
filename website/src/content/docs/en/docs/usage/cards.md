title: Cards
description: How the cards beside the conversation are arranged and sized.

  order: 2
---

While ASIST answers by voice, it shows the weather, events, mail drafts, to-dos, exchange rates, news, maps, timers and more on cards beside the conversation.

ASIST shows at most two cards, one on each side. When there is no free space, the new card takes the place of the card that has been showing the longest. From the top right of a card, you can enlarge it or close it.

Cards switch between three sizes to fit the height of the window. Whatever does not fit is shown in the focus view, through a line such as "3 more". Cards fit even in the smallest window (a height of 640).
